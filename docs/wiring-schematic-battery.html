On-Board Surgery Checklist (MS309-3 V:1.5)

  • ✂ CUT #1: transceiver pin 1 (TXD) trace → old scanner MCU (prevents bus fights)
  • ✂ CUT #2: transceiver pin 3 (VCC) trace → AMS1117 output (transceiver now runs off ESP32 3V3)
  • JUMPER: pin 1 → GPIO 5 · pin 4 → GPIO 4 · pin 3 → ESP32 3V3 · pin 8 (RS) → GND
  • VERIFY: transceiver pin 3 must read 3.3 V ±0.1 while powered — if ~5 V the chip is a 5 V family and GPIO 4 needs protection
  • ORIENT: the tiny white dot on the transceiver marks pin 1 (top-left corner) — TXD
  • KEEP: rainbow plug installed · orange → CANH · yellow → CANL · black/blue → GND · purple (+12 V) left unconnected
